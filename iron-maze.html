Sector 7 bunker

Iron Maze

Security bots have taken over the bunker. Eight of them patrol its rooms and corridors. Shut every one down. Green barrels explode, so keep your distance or wait for a bot to walk past one.

On a phone or tablet? Iron Maze needs a keyboard and mouse, so open it on a computer to play.

How the 3D works. The bunker is a flat 24 × 24 grid of walls and floor. Every frame, the game sends out 480 rays, one per column of pixels, and draws each wall slice taller the closer its ray hit. The fan of lines on the map is those rays.
