Five levels

Iron Maze

Security bots have taken over the bunker. Fight down through five levels, from the outer halls to the reactor core. Each level is bigger and darker, the bots get sharper, and two tougher models join along the way. Green barrels explode.

On a phone or tablet? Iron Maze needs a keyboard and mouse, so open it on a computer to play.

How the 3D works. Each level is a flat grid of walls and floor, typed into the code as rows of characters. Every frame, the game sends out 480 rays, one per column of pixels, and draws each wall slice taller the closer its ray hit. The fan of lines on the map is those rays.
