Five levels

Iron Maze

Security bots have taken over the bunker. Fight down through five levels, from the outer halls to the reactor core. Each level is bigger and darker, the bots get sharper, and two tougher models join along the way. Green barrels explode.

On a phone or tablet? Iron Maze needs a keyboard and mouse, so open it on a computer to play.

Loading the 3D engine...

How the 3D works. Each level is still a flat grid of characters typed into the code. When a level loads, the game turns every wall character into a 3D block, works out where the lamp light falls, and builds the robots from simple shapes. Your graphics card then draws the whole scene about 60 times a second.
